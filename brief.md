# Patient Assessment Surface

## Scope and Mode
Scope: Complete patient assessment flow (Welcome, Symptoms, Context, Analyzing, Result).
Mode: Operate.

## Target Audience and Job
Patients feeling unwell, seeking a calm, authoritative, and structured assessment of their symptoms to know what to do next.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A highly clinical, minimal, and deterministic interface that refuses playful or "AI-like" ambiguity in favor of Apple Health-style clarity and precision.
OWN-WORLD: Stark white backgrounds, crisp and highly structured geometry (sharp corners or tight 4px/8px radii), deep navy text, and a single strategic accent color (Healthcare Green or Sky Blue) for primary actions. No soft shadows, no pill shapes, no bubbly layouts.
STORY: The user feels they are using a serious, medical-grade diagnostic tool. They trust the process because it is transparent, bounded, and structured.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A stark, clean welcome screen. Deep Navy crisp typography on a pure white canvas. A sharp, rectangular primary CTA anchoring the bottom. No floating blob shapes or bubbly borders.
FORM: User-pinned (Clinical & Minimal / Crisp & Structured / Monochrome). Seed key a1fee57f overridden by explicit user preference.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
